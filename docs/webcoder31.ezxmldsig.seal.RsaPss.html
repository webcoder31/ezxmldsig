    Class webcoder31\ezxmldsig\seal\RsaPss

    RSASSA-PSS (RFC 8017, 8.1), the signature scheme of the seals for an RSA key, with MGF1 over the digest of the message (9.1, B.2.1).

    openssl_sign() and openssl_verify() take no padding parameter: they sign and verify with PKCS#1 v1.5 only. The encoding EMSA-PSS is therefore written here, and the RSA operation itself is left to OpenSSL, without padding (OPENSSL_NO_PADDING): openssl_private_encrypt() computes the signature of the encoded message, openssl_public_decrypt() gives back the encoded message of a signature. The encoded message is one bit shorter than the modulus, its leftmost bits cleared: as a number, it is smaller than the modulus, which OpenSSL requires.
