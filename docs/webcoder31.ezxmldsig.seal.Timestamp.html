    Class webcoder31\ezxmldsig\seal\Timestamp

    Time-stamps (RFC 3161, updated by RFC 5816): the request, the response, and the verification of a time-stamp token, a CMS SignedData (RFC 5652) whose content is a TSTInfo.

    No network access: the caller sends the request to the time-stamping authority of its choice. The token is checked as a signature of its own: one signer, the signed attributes that bind the content and the certificate of the authority (ESS signing-certificate, RFC 2634 and RFC 5035), the extended key usage timeStamping of that certificate (RFC 3161, 2.3), the algorithms of the seals. Whether the authority is trusted is decided by the caller, with the certificate returned.
