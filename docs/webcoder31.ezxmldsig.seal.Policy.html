    Class webcoder31\ezxmldsig\seal\Policy

    The algorithms of the seals: their digests and signature schemes, the curves of the EC keys, the smallest RSA key. The identifiers are those of the core; the digests are those of the tokens, SHA-1 and RIPEMD-160 excepted (ETSI TS 119 312).
