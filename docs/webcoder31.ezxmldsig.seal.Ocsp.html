    Class webcoder31\ezxmldsig\seal\Ocsp

    OCSP (RFC 6960): the request of the status of a certificate, the reading of a response, and the verification of its responder.

    The request identifies the certificate by SHA-1 hashes of the name and of the key of its issuer, as the lightweight profile requires (RFC 5019, 2.1.1) and as responders expect: an identifier, not a signature. It has no nonce: the responses of the seals are kept, and the freshness rules of Revocation, not a nonce, tell whether one is current.

    A response is signed by the CA that issued the certificate, or by a responder that CA delegated (RFC 6960, 4.2.2.2): a certificate issued directly by it, of extended key usage id-kp-OCSPSigning. The delegated responder must carry id-pkix-ocsp-nocheck (4.2.2.2.1): this library does not check the revocation of responders.
