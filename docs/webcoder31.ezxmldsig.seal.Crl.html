    Class webcoder31\ezxmldsig\seal\Crl

    Certificate revocation lists (RFC 5280, 5): their reading, the verification of their signature by the CA that issued the certificate, and the status of a certificate.

    Only complete, direct CRLs are used: a delta CRL, an indirect CRL, a CRL restricted to some reasons, or a critical extension this class does not know, make the CRL unusable rather than partly read.

    A CRL read is an array: 'der', 'issuer' (the DER of its issuer name), 'thisUpdate', 'nextUpdate' (UNIX times, NULL if none), 'revoked' (by the serial number in hexadecimal: 'date' and 'reason'), 'onlyUserCerts', 'onlyCACerts', 'expiredCertsOnCrl', 'tbs', 'signatureAlgorithm', 'signature'.
